Data and privacy

Your planner, your choices. Updated 13 October 2026.

Choose where your planner lives

Device-only mode keeps your schedules, notes, habits, focus records, reminders and preferences in this browser. It does not upload your planner to a Weekwise account. Clearing browser storage can remove it, so download a backup first.

Account mode sends your planner snapshot to the connected Supabase backend so you can reload it on another device. The cloud stores your latest revision; account changes and conflicts create local recovery copies. Each account can read only its own cloud records.

Online page assets are served by GitHub Pages and a JavaScript CDN. Authentication and account services use Supabase. These hosting services can process ordinary network and operational logs.

Authentication and invitations

Supabase handles your email, password authentication and sessions. Weekwise keeps the session in browser storage to maintain sign-in. An invitation is checked against a private hash before a new account is created. Newly submitted invitation values are removed from the new account's metadata after verification.

Signing out stops automatic account use on this device. A previously issued access token can remain valid until it expires; refresh sessions are revoked separately. Account erasure requires your password again.

Optional background reminders

Background reminders need your browser's notification permission and an enabled device subscription. The server stores the push endpoint, browser-generated subscription keys, time zone and device information. It checks reminders in your cloud planner and uses a delivery log to prevent duplicate sends.

The push provider receives the notification payload, including the task title, task identifier and reminder time. A notification title can appear on your lock screen. Disable background reminders in Reminder settings to remove that device's server subscription, and use browser or system settings to revoke permission. Account erasure removes the account's subscriptions and delivery records.

Retention and recovery copies

Your local planner and account caches remain until you deliberately clear them or the browser removes storage. Archives, Recently removed records, full-editor and quick-edit drafts, task history, imports and conversion checkpoints have no automatic purge. The cloud snapshot remains until overwritten or the account is erased. Delivery logs have no app-defined automatic purge.

Backups and reviews you download contain readable private planner data. Keep them somewhere you trust. They exclude authentication tokens and server credentials. Files you download, copies on other devices, system notifications and hosting-provider backups or operational logs are outside this browser's cleanup controls.

Interface preferences and quick edits

Pinned lists are part of your planner preferences and sync with a connected account. Expanded navigation groups are remembered on this device for each account. Quick-edit drafts also stay on this device; they can be restored, downloaded or discarded from the task list. They are separate from normal planner exports. Account erasure removes that account’s stored navigation preferences and quick-edit drafts from this browser after the server confirms deletion.

Export, restore or erase

Use Settings → Data & privacy to export your planner, inspect archives or restore a reviewed backup. Restore creates a checkpoint and refuses to overwrite a newer planner without review. A time-zone conversion offers an original download and a full review before applying changes.

For permanent erasure, use the account-erasure control, download any records you want to retain, type the confirmation phrase and enter your current password. After server confirmation, Weekwise clears the recognized local entries for that account on this device. Other accounts and device-only records are preserved. Copies on your other devices require cleanup there.

Calendars, AI and measurements

Google and Outlook account connections are deferred and are not active. Calendar file import and export work locally. No optional AI provider is configured, and your planner is not sent to an AI provider by Weekwise. Planning suggestions use the app's deterministic rules.

Performance and accessibility checks use controlled test fixtures. Weekwise does not add a remote analytics collector for those checks. Automated checks do not establish human screen-reader certification, physical notification delivery or real-user Core Web Vitals.

Questions about your account

Contact the Weekwise owner who provided your invitation for account administration or hosting-retention questions. This page describes the application's current data behavior and controls.
