Data and privacy

Your planner, your choices. Updated 14 October 2026.

Choose where your planner lives

Device-only mode keeps your schedules, notes, habits, focus records, reminders, optional personal name and date of birth, and preferences in this browser. It does not upload your planner to a Weekwise account. Clearing browser storage can remove it, so download a backup first.

Account mode sends your planner snapshot—including any optional name and date of birth saved in Settings → Personal details—to the connected Supabase backend so you can reload it on another device. Your saved name appears only in the private Today greeting when the optional greeting setting is enabled; you can turn this off without removing the name. These details are not used to calculate age or displayed on public pages. The cloud stores your latest revision; account changes and conflicts create local recovery copies. Each account can read only its own cloud records.

Online page assets are served by GitHub Pages and a JavaScript CDN. Authentication and account services use Supabase. These hosting services can process ordinary network and operational logs.

Authentication and invitations

Supabase handles your email, password authentication and sessions. Weekwise keeps the session in browser storage to maintain sign-in. An invitation is checked against a private hash before a new account is created. Newly submitted invitation values are removed from the new account's metadata after verification.

Signing out stops automatic account use on this device. A previously issued access token can remain valid until it expires; refresh sessions are revoked separately. Account erasure requires your password again.

Optional background reminders

Background reminders need your browser's notification permission and an enabled device subscription. The server stores the push endpoint, browser-generated subscription keys, time zone and device information. It checks reminders in your cloud planner and uses a delivery log to prevent duplicate sends.

The push provider receives the notification payload, including the task title, task identifier and reminder time. A notification title can appear on your lock screen. Disable background reminders in Reminder settings to remove that device's server subscription, and use browser or system settings to revoke permission. Account erasure removes the account's subscriptions and delivery records.

Retention and recovery copies

Your local planner and account caches remain until you deliberately clear them or the browser removes storage. Archives, Recently removed records, full-editor, quick-edit and weekly-review drafts, task history, imports and conversion checkpoints have no automatic purge. Weekly review recovery drafts are stored only in this browser for each account and week, excluded from normal backups, and downloadable separately from Settings. Up to 20 unfinished weeks are retained without automatic eviction; saving or explicitly discarding a draft removes that week’s recovery copy. Saved reviews are part of the planner and its normal backups/account sync. The cloud snapshot remains until overwritten or the account is erased. Delivery logs have no app-defined automatic purge.

Backups and reviews you download contain readable private planner data. Keep them somewhere you trust. They exclude authentication tokens and server credentials. Files you download, copies on other devices, system notifications and hosting-provider backups or operational logs are outside this browser's cleanup controls.

Device checks and optional speed measurements

Device confirmations and optional performance history stay in this browser for the current account. Recording defaults to off and begins on a future reload after you opt in. History keeps at most 80 numeric page samples and 50 view-response measurements per sample. It includes build, day and viewport class, without task contents, authentication tokens, URLs or interaction targets. No analytics service receives these records. Stop recording or clear its history in Settings → Device, speed & recovery. A report is downloaded only when you choose to export it.

Backup rehearsal reads a file you select into an isolated memory store. The file is not uploaded, and rehearsal does not replace the planner, cloud data or existing recovery checkpoints. Reports contain checksum and collection counts without task contents. Confirmed cloud-account erasure removes that account’s local measurement and device-confirmation keys; downloaded reports and other device copies remain under your control.

Optional troubleshooting history

Troubleshooting recording is off by default. When enabled in Settings → Device, speed & recovery, it stores at most 80 fixed-code cloud-check, cloud-save and app-error outcomes on this device for this account. Events contain only day, build, outcome and an optional numeric duration. Error messages, task contents, account identities, tokens, URLs and stack traces are excluded. Nothing is uploaded. Stop or clear recording in the same panel; account erasure removes that account’s recognized diagnostic key on this device. A device report includes these events only when you download it.

Public service monitoring uses GitHub Actions to make periodic read-only requests to public service endpoints and the published app. Its summaries contain fixed check names and pass/fail outcomes, without account sessions or planner records. This does not measure your individual activity or prove mail or phone notification delivery. The optional first-run guide stores its shown, started or dismissed preference locally for each planner and derives progress from existing saved records.

Interface preferences and quick edits

Pinned lists are part of your planner preferences and sync with a connected account. Expanded navigation groups are remembered on this device for each account. Quick-edit drafts also stay on this device; they can be restored, downloaded or discarded from the task list. They are separate from normal planner exports. Account erasure removes that account’s stored navigation preferences and quick-edit drafts from this browser after the server confirms deletion.

Export, restore or erase

Use Settings → Data & privacy for JSON backup export, confirmed restore, task-list file transfers and recovery downloads in device-only or signed-in mode. Restoring a file in account mode may replace your synced cloud planner, so review it carefully and keep a separate copy when needed. The calendar .ics file controls are also available only in Settings → Calendars. You can still inspect archives and recovery controls. Restore creates a checkpoint and refuses to overwrite a newer planner without review. A time-zone conversion offers a complete review and local checkpoint before apply; device-only mode also offers original and review downloadsing changes.

For permanent erasure, use the account-erasure control, download any records you want to retain, type the confirmation phrase and enter your current password. After server confirmation, Weekwise clears the recognized local entries for that account on this device. Other accounts and device-only records are preserved. Copies on your other devices require cleanup there.

Calendars, AI and measurements

Google and Outlook calendar connections are optional and require provider setup followed by your explicit account consent. When connected, Weekwise stores encrypted access and refresh tokens, provider account information and event links in its server-only backend. A manual sync reads events from your primary calendar and creates or updates eligible scheduled Weekwise activities there, within 30 days back and 90 days ahead. Task titles, notes and event times are shared with the connected provider during sync. Conflicting edits or linked-event deletions are reported for review. Disconnect removes Weekwise’s stored credentials and links; existing calendar events remain, and provider consent must be revoked in the provider account settings. Account erasure removes stored calendar connections and links. Calendar file import and export work locally. No optional AI provider is configured, and your planner is not sent to an AI provider by Weekwise. Planning suggestions use the app's deterministic rules.

Performance and accessibility checks use controlled test fixtures. Weekwise does not add a remote analytics collector for those checks. Automated checks do not establish human screen-reader certification, physical notification delivery or real-user Core Web Vitals.
