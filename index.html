GOOD TO KNOW

A few questions, answered.

Do I need an account to use Weekwise?+

No. Choose “Explore on this device” to use a local planner. Your changes stay in this browser. You can also sign in to an existing account to use account-based sync.

Can I use Weekwise on my phone?+

Yes. Weekwise has responsive calendar, task and planning views. It is also installable as a web app on supported browsers.

Does Weekwise automatically schedule everything?+

No. You choose what to put on the calendar. The week planner offers suggestions for review, and applying a plan is a deliberate step. Tasks in your Inbox do not become appointments on their own.

Can I create a cloud account?+

Weekwise supports sign-in and account creation with an invite code. Cloud features depend on the configured backend and your connection; device-only planning works separately.

Does it connect to Google or Outlook calendars?+

Calendar connection controls are available, but connecting external providers requires their separate account and backend configuration. Weekwise does not claim those connections are active before they are configured.

        Why are my devices showing different plans?

        Sign in to the same account on both devices and check Account & sync. A device-only planner is separate from your account planner. Save open work and update both devices when a new version is offered.

        While the app is open, Weekwise checks for changes periodically and when you reconnect or return to the app. Independent edits can combine. If both devices change the same record, sync pauses for review.

        If you see Needs review, export each copy before choosing which version to keep.

        Can I work offline?

        Open Weekwise online first so the app can cache its files. An installed app can then reopen offline when the browser supports it. Choose your saved account copy to continue that account offline; its edits upload when you sign in again and reconnect.

        Device-only mode stays in this browser. Clearing browser storage can remove it, so keep a downloaded backup.

        Why did a reminder not arrive?

        Check system and browser notification permission, enable background reminders for this device, and confirm that the reminder reached your cloud planner. Some phone browsers need home-screen installation.

        A subscription being ready does not prove delivery. Schedule a harmless test reminder, close all Weekwise tabs, and check for a system notification. Foreground reminders require the app to remain open.

        How do I protect or restore my data?

        Export a backup before major changes. Keep the file somewhere private. Settings → Device, speed & recovery can rehearse a copy without replacing your planner.

        To verify a full restore, use a separate browser profile in device-only mode. Import a copy, review tasks and history, reload, then export again. Restore in your main planner replaces included collections after confirmation and keeps a recovery checkpoint.

        Keyboard and accessibility

        Use Ctrl or ⌘ + K to capture from the planner. Use Tab and Shift + Tab to move between controls, Enter to activate, and Esc to close a dialog. An unfinished editor asks before closing.

        Five palettes and device appearance are available under Appearance in Settings. Browser zoom is supported. For a problem with a screen reader, note the screen and action so it can be reproduced.

        Accounts, calendars and privacy

        Your invitation lets you create an account. For confirmation or password-reset emails, check inbox and spam. If a link has expired, request a new one. The person who invited you can help with account administration.

        Google and Outlook use separate calendar connections after the Weekwise owner completes provider setup. Connect the account you want to use and approve calendar access. Calendar files remain available. Planning suggestions use deterministic rules; no AI service receives your planner.
