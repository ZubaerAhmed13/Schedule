Focus rules

Keep the system helpful and forgiving.

1
Three priorities a day. One career task, one learning task, and one life task is enough.
2
Use focus sprints. Try 50 minutes of work and a 10-minute break, or start with 25 minutes.
3
Make outcomes specific. Complete one tailored application, review one lecture, or speak German aloud.
4
Don't punish a missed day. Resume at the next planned block; don't double tomorrow's workload.
5
Protect your city day. Treat the 9-hour trip as part of a balanced life, not lost productivity.
